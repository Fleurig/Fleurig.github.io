Timelotus

World clocks in your Mac menu bar.

Timelotus puts the time of any city right in your menu bar, next to your own clock. See your agenda in another time zone, plan calls with time travel, and add widgets to your desktop.

Support

Questions, ideas or found a bug? Email fleurigdesign@gmail.com.

Where is the app? Timelotus lives in the menu bar at the top right of your screen. Click the time to open it.

Widgets: right-click your desktop → Edit Widgets → search "Timelotus". To choose the agenda widget's time zone, right-click it → Edit.

Calendar: turn on "Show calendar agenda" in Settings. You can allow or remove access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.
